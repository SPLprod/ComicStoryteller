What is Comic Storyteller ?

ComicStoryteller is a web app in which comics artists / mangaka can train their artistic & storytelling skills with random scenarios in a arcade environment.

Goal

It's simple, I want to be an author and I wanna train to make comics/mangas so here's a tool for training easily without thinking much about making scenarios and also challenging oneself on diverse restrictions and diverse specific goal to achieve.

Technology used

Just simple Javascript and a lot of trial and errors.
